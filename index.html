Capstone · Jan – May 2026

AI-Powered Threat Intelligence Dashboard

n8nStreamlitPython AlienVault OTXNVD CVEKrebs on Security

Problem

SOC analysts drown in raw threat feeds. The signal — which indicators actually matter right now — gets buried across half a dozen disconnected sources.

Approach

Built automated n8n workflows that collect and enrich threat data from AlienVault OTX, Krebs on Security, and the NVD CVE feeds — extracting and categorizing IOCs including IPs, domains, URLs, and file hashes.

Delivery

Designed a Streamlit SOC console with seven analyst views — Analytics, Origin Map, IOCs, Threat Table, Deep Dive, Alerts, and Export. Analysts filter live threats by severity, source, and attack type and work them from a priority queue.

Analytics

The landscape view breaks threats down by severity (Critical → Low), ranks the top attack types — credential theft, malware, exploits, backdoors, data exfiltration — and surfaces the most-affected software, so the highest-impact items rise to the top on their own.

Outcome

Collapses three noisy feeds into a single triage surface an analyst can act on — the difference between a data dump and a work queue.

AI · Computer Vision + LLM · Team Project

Forked — AI Meal Suggestion App

YOLO (custom-trained)UltralyticsOllama deepseek-r1Node.jsFlaskHugging Face

Problem

You open a full fridge and still don't know what to cook that fits your macros. Forked turns a photo of your ingredients into meals aligned to your fitness and nutrition goals.

Vision

A custom-trained YOLO object-detection model (Ultralytics) reads uploaded fridge / ingredient photos and returns a de-duplicated ingredient list.

Generation

Those ingredients feed a local Ollama LLM (deepseek-r1) that generates goal-aligned recipes — no external API, the model runs on-box.

Ship

Full stack: a Node.js interface with multi-image drag-and-drop over a Python/Flask detection API. Deployed live on Hugging Face.

Self-directed · Network & Infrastructure Security

Cybersecurity Home Lab — Segmented Network

OPNsenseVLANs (802.1Q)WireGuard VPN Zero TrustWPA2Subnetting

Goal

Build an enterprise-style segmented network for a small-business simulation — isolating management traffic from user traffic the way a real corporate network does.

Perimeter

An OPNsense firewall (Intel N100) handling WAN/LAN, a Deny-All WAN ingress policy, and a WireGuard VPN as the only whitelisted way in.

Segmentation

802.1Q VLAN tagging: a Management VLAN (10) carried over a trunked uplink with access ports for endpoints, pulled off the default VLAN 1 — a Zero-Trust admin segment.

Wireless

Access point run in AP mode (NAT/DHCP disabled) to centralize routing and logging, prevent double-NAT, and keep WPA2 traffic auditable.
